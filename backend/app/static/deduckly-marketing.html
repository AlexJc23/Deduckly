A few useful answers

Get to know Deduckly.

Who is Deduckly for?

Deduckly helps gig workers, delivery drivers, rideshare drivers, and other independent workers organize work mileage, income, and expenses in one app.

How does GPS mileage tracking work?

Start a trip in Deduckly to record mileage using your device’s location. Location access is needed for tracking, including background access to continue recording an active trip when the app is in the background.

What does the Offer Analyzer calculate?

Enter an offer’s payout, total distance, and estimated time. The free Offer Analyzer shows estimated profit, hourly earnings, and dollars per mile. Deduckly Pro unlocks deeper control over personal preferences for your analysis. Results depend on your inputs and vehicle costs; they are estimates, not guaranteed earnings.

Is Deduckly available for iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Download Deduckly from the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Is Deduckly free to download?

Deduckly is free to download, and the Offer Analyzer is free to use. An optional Deduckly Pro subscription unlocks deeper personal preference controls and other Pro features. Check the app for current plans, pricing, and included features.
